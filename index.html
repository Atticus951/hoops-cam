Each bounce should push a bar past the black line once. Missing bounces: slide up. Counting extra: slide down.

Setup tips
  1. Prop the tablet 6 to 10 feet away with the screen facing him, so the front camera sees him from head to floor.
  2. Tap Draw zones. Drag the right-hand box first, then the left. The picture is mirrored, so his right hand shows on the right side of the screen.
  3. Draw each box low, from the floor to about knee height, where the ball bounces. Keep the two boxes apart.
  4. Dribble a few times and watch for the R or L flash. Adjust sensitivity until each bounce flashes once.
