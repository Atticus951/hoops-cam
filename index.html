A bar passing the black line means the camera saw movement there. A basket counts when Rim fires and then Net fires right after.

Setup tips
  1. Prop the tablet 6 to 10 feet from the hoop with the camera you're using pointed at it. Upright or sideways both work. Use the front camera so he can see the score, or the back camera if the front one is blurry.
  2. Drag a box from just above the rim to the bottom of the net. Yellow watches the rim, orange watches the net.
  3. Keep people and pets out of the box between shots. A plain wall behind the hoop helps.
  4. If it misses baskets, slide sensitivity up. If it counts misses, slide it down.
