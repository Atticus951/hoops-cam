Setup tips
  1. Put the tablet on a shelf or stand 6 to 10 feet from the hoop, using the back camera, so the whole rim and net are in view.
  2. Drag a box from just above the rim down to the bottom of the net. The yellow top part watches the rim, the orange part watches the net.
  3. Keep people and pets out of the box when no one's shooting. A plain wall behind the hoop works best.
  4. Take a few shots. If it misses baskets, slide sensitivity up. If it counts misses, slide it down.
