The bars show where the ball is in each box: full at the floor, empty at the top. One trip down and back up counts as one dribble. Missing dribbles: slide up. Counting extra: slide down.

Setup tips
  1. Prop the tablet 6 to 10 feet away with the screen facing him, so the front camera sees him from head to floor.
  2. Tap the screen for the menu, then Draw zones. Drag the right-hand box first, then the left. The picture is mirrored, so his right hand shows on the right side of the screen.
  3. Draw each box from the floor up to about the height of the bounce, where the ball travels. Keep the two boxes apart.
  4. Dribble a few times and watch for the R or L flash, once per dribble. Adjust sensitivity if needed.
